🐙 How This Works

Post your game results in the channel — Wordle, Queens, Krillion, whatever you played. They show up here automatically, no manual entry.

Your Standings rank is your average finish position across every game you play — like a golf score, lower is better. Krillion, Wordle, and Queens count for more than the rest. Play more games, and your average just shifts toward however you did there.

Everything locks in at 20:00 each day — that's when the Standings update and the daily creature raffle is drawn.
