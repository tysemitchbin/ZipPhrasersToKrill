🐙 How This Works

Points come from three places — skill, effort, and luck — so showing up every day keeps you in it even on a cold streak.

🧠 Skill: each day, each game, players are ranked by score (some games are lowest-is-best like Wordle, others highest-is-best). The winner gets 4, last gets 1, everyone spaced evenly between — the same whether 4 or 12 people played. Ties share rank and points. A game only counts if at least 4 people played it that day.

💪 Effort: play every game that counted today for a full-sweep bonus, and keep a daily play streak going (any game counts) — 3, 7, 14, 30… days each pay out.

🎰 Luck — Roulette: the bottom third of the day each spin a Mario Kart-style wheel at 16:00 (last place spins twice) — mostly a boost, occasionally a banana peel.

🌟 Luck — Milestones: land your all-time total exactly on a special number — a palindrome (121, 2332), a repdigit (222), a digit-run (123, 456), or a certified classic (69, 420, 1337).

🎲 A different unhinged mascot announces the bonuses each day (today it's …). Everything's logged automatically by the group's Discord bot the second someone posts their share text — no manual entry.
