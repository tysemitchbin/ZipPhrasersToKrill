How this works

Each day, for each game, whoever played gets ranked by their score (some games rank lowest-is-best, like Wordle guesses; others rank highest-is-best). The best score earns points equal to however many people played that game that day; each place below that earns one point less. Ties share the same rank and points. A player's daily total is the sum across every game they played that day — skipping a game just means no points from it, not a penalty.

Scores are logged automatically by the group's Discord bot when someone posts their share-text (or a plain Game: score message) in the scores channel.
